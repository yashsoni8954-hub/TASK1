About Us

InAmigos Foundation is a registered non-profit organization based in Chhattisgarh, India, founded in 2020. We work directly with underprivileged families, children, women, and animals in need — bringing food, education, healthcare, and support to communities that need it most.

Our goal is simple: to create real, lasting change through small, consistent acts of service. We also partner with companies to help them fulfill their CSR (Corporate Social Responsibility) goals in a transparent and measurable way.

Our Ongoing Projects

Project Seva

Providing food, clothing, and basic essentials to families facing hardship.

Project Bachpanshala

Supporting education for underprivileged children through school supplies and after-school learning support.

Project Jeev

Caring for stray and injured animals through feeding, shelter, and veterinary support.

Project Udaan

Empowering women through skill-building workshops and vocational training so they can earn independently.

Project Prakriti

Promoting environmental sustainability through tree plantation drives and cleanliness campaigns.

Social Impact

Every project we run is designed to create change that lasts beyond a single day of giving:

  • Immediate relief reaches families quickly when a need is identified.
  • Education and skill-building create opportunities that continue for years.
  • Local volunteers guide our work, so it reflects what communities actually need.
